Changelog: v.1.29.00

[Added] Many new zombies now spawn around buildings.
[Added] All zombies can now carry special, particularly valuable items, making looting zombies worthwhile again. These include hotwire kits, suitcase bombs, hemp, dog tags, and much more.
[Added] The Holy Book can now be read.
[Added] Zombies can now carry backpacks. These backpacks sometimes contain items.
[Added] All rocket launchers can now also be found in military building loot.
[Added] The key for a vehicle can now also be deleted using the scroll option.
[Added] Players who have found the black market can now mark it globally for all other players by looking at the trader and selecting Mark Black Market.
[Added] Several buildings now have new additional loot positions.
[Changed] Zombies can now be hidden again.
[Changed] The zombie limit has been increased.
[Changed] Players can now access and get into bicycles, helicopters, and other vehicles much faster after deploying them.
[Changed] Non-military weapons have been removed from the loot tables for military loot.
[Changed] Toilet houses no longer spawn gems as loot.
[Changed] Valuable items can spawn in residential and office loot.
[Changed] The RP for zombies has been increased from 5 to 10 RP.
[Changed] Zombies can now carry up to 6,000 coins.
[Changed] AIs now generally have fewer items in their inventory.
[Changed] The markers for convoys on the map should now update more quickly.
[Changed] The loot from randomly spawning ammo crates on the map has been reworked.
[Changed] Erzadern von AI-Missionen sind nun mit dem Vorschlaghammer einfacher abzubauen als Erzadern, die nicht in den AI-Missionen spawnen.
[Fixed] The maximum equipment slots that can be carried by the Wildcat AH11 and Apache AH1 were not displayed correctly in the trader menu and trader catalog.
[Fixed] Large items could also spawn on small loot positions.
[Fixed] Ore veins could spawn on roads.
